## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Gulf Coast Mesh App is a free, open-source companion app for MeshCore LoRa mesh radios — off-grid, encrypted communication that works with no cell service, no Wi-Fi, and no internet connection required.

Built by the Gulf Coast Mesh community, Gulf Coast Mesh App connects to your MeshCore-compatible device over Bluetooth and gives you a fast, clean interface for staying in touch with your mesh network wherever you are — from neighborhood check-ins to backcountry trips to emergency preparedness.

FEATURES

• Direct & group messaging — chat with contacts and channels over LoRa radio, with full message history stored locally on your device
• Live mesh map — see nodes and repeaters on your network in real time
• Contact & channel management — add contacts, join channels, and organize your mesh community from one hub
• Repeater & room server admin — manage repeater settings and logins directly from the app
• ATAK location sharing (optional) — broadcast your GPS position to your mesh channel for team awareness
• Firmware updates — check for and install the latest MeshCore firmware releases over BLE
• Line-of-sight terrain tool — check the elevation profile between two points before planning a link
• GIF search and link previews (optional) — a little fun and readability without giving up control of your data

PRIVACY FIRST

Your messages, contacts, and channel keys are stored locally on your device — passwords and secret keys are protected with hardware-backed secure storage. Gulf Coast Mesh App has no ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs, and we never sell your data. Optional features like wardriving, GIF search, and link previews only send data off your device when you explicitly turn them on. Full privacy policy: gulfcoastmesh.org/privacy

OPEN SOURCE

Gulf Coast Mesh App is fully open source — audit the code, file issues, or contribute at github.com/Cloud-121/gulfcoastmesh-app

Gulf Coast Mesh App requires a compatible MeshCore LoRa radio device to connect to a mesh network.
```
