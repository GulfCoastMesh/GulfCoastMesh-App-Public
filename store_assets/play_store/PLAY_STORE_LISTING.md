## Full description

Gulf Coast Mesh App is an off-grid messaging client for MeshCore-compatible radios.
Pair your phone with a compatible radio over Bluetooth, then communicate
across your local LoRa mesh—without relying on cell service or Wi-Fi.

Built for community networks, outdoor trips, events, and everyday
preparedness, Gulf Coast Mesh App keeps the people and nodes you depend on close at
hand.

With Gulf Coast Mesh App you can:

- Send direct messages and follow delivery activity
- Join community, private, and hashtag channels
- Discover nearby contacts, favorite key nodes, and view node details
- See contacts and repeaters on a map
- Share compatible tactical-map positions and points of interest with your
  channel
- Manage supported radio settings and tools from your phone

## Your mesh, your messages

Gulf Coast Mesh App is designed around MeshCore radio networks. Messages and optional
location shares travel over the radio mesh, not through Gulf Coast Mesh App servers.
Location broadcasting is optional and off by default.

## What you need

A MeshCore-compatible radio running compatible firmware and a
Bluetooth-capable Android phone. Radio range and performance depend on
terrain, antenna setup, local settings, and available mesh nodes.
